Create from your own AI — the campaign-creation skill

NEW · AI-NATIVE

A "campaign creation" skill that campaign creators can install in their Claude or in their own agent — the external version of having an AI assistant. The skill knows Rally’s rules and best practices, drafts name, ask, rules and knowledge base in chat, and hands off a pre-filled /create link: the user lands on the one-page form with everything ready, reviews, and deposits. Inside the app, Wingston plays the same role (the "AI Template" and "Draft with AI" buttons); the skill takes campaign creation to wherever the creator already works.

6

Creator vs Brand — same path, different face

THE DISTINCTION
🎨 As a CREATOR
Dual role: participates as a user AND can own an organization
  • Plays both sides of Rally: participates in other people’s campaigns (Rules → Post → Get paid, flow 03) and also launches their own with their organization — same account, same wallet. That’s the difference vs a brand.
  • Organization in 30 seconds: pfp, name and bio pre-filled from their personal X; banner and links later. Born not verified.
  • Verified: usually NO — skips the question in one click. No Community (a Verified perk). Can verify if they grow.
  • Wallet: the one they already use to participate and get paid. If they came in via Metamask, this step never shows.
  • Pool in RLP or USDC. Their campaign lands on the NETWORK rail, org shown without badge.
  • Total: ~2 minutes from entry to live.
🚀 As a BRAND
Organization side only: launches campaigns, doesn’t participate
  • Operates only as an organization: launches and manages campaigns — doesn’t post into other people’s campaigns. Their account exists for their project.
  • Organization with the project’s X: same step, same screen; fills in banner, description and links. Born not verified.
  • Verified: their call. YES → video call with Ana + $1,500 → blue badge, priority placement, promotion from Rally’s official channels, featured rail, own Community, custom token/points, invoice. NO → launches on Network just like a creator, free and without Community.
  • Wallet: same mechanics. One per account; team wallets can be added later as admins.
  • Can launch while the review runs — starts on Network and moves up to the Verified rail when the badge lands.
  • Same account mechanics, different use: there is no special brand account — the difference is a brand only uses the organization side, and a creator uses both.

05 · retention — verified organizations only

Elite Creators

The feature already lives in the Community (tabs: Overview · Campaigns · Elite Creators · Members · Posts) — so it’s Verified orgs only. The official copy rules: "Official Elite Creators represent the org across their channels. Any community member can apply when applications are open" — membership is the requirement, and membership is automatic (earn points in one of the org’s campaigns → you’re in). The payment model is chosen per exclusive campaign (block 3 of the form, when "Elite creators" is selected): fixed pay per post (pay per post + max posts per creator, or upload a list) or curve distribution (the pool splits across the Elite Creators through the reward curve). Exclusive campaigns only — public ones always pay through the curve. With fixed pay, the AI verifies every post → if true, the payment is approved but held until the campaign ends → automatic release. Design rule: Rally already has the data (Sorsa, followers, history) → zero forms.

how it all connects

The Loop

01 Anyone creates an org (not verified) and launches on Network → 02 their campaign shows up in the feed → 03 creators participate, get paid and see "create yours" → back to 01. In parallel: 04 serious orgs go Verified ($1,500) → featured rail + Community → 05 they run their Elite Creator Program and retain their best creators — who bring more creators into the feed. Each flow feeds the next; the $1,500 and campaign fees fund the system, and submissions (100 RLP) move the north star.
