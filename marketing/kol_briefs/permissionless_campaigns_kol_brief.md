# CAMPAIGN BRIEF — PERMISSIONLESS CAMPAIGNS

## 1. Context
Distribution is the hardest problem in crypto. You can build a great product or launch a token, but getting real attention means agencies, sales calls, KOL round-ups and unpredictable results. Rally just shipped the fix — **Permissionless Campaigns**, its biggest release to date: now anyone can create a marketing campaign on Rally. No sales call. No agency. No permission.

## 2. What is RALLY?
Rally is the first AI-powered, onchain marketing protocol where anyone can earn by posting on X. Built on GenLayer, it's one of the most active protocols in consumer crypto. Creators post, AI validators (GPT, Claude, Grok) score every entry onchain, and winners get paid automatically from escrow. For projects and devs, Rally is the first **decentralized marketing protocol** that actually solves distribution — and it runs the **lowest CPM in all of crypto**, because you only pay for verified results. And Rally keeps shipping while the rest of the space went quiet: communities inside Rally, clipping campaigns, and now permissionless campaigns.
